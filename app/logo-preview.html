Logo Evolution

The creative process behind the Inspirations lightbulb — from muddy square to Edison bulb with vector rays.
1

Gold-to-sage gradient square

The original placeholder logo — a simple rounded rectangle with a linear gradient.

"Gold-to-sage isn't really discernible. The gold just comes off muddy."

2

Dark charcoal with white outline bulb

Attempted a high-contrast approach with white line art on dark background.

"Logo is a regression now."

3

Warm stone background with dark bulb

Lighter background, dark charcoal bulb outline with amber accent. User shared a Vecteezy reference of a stylized yellow Edison bulb — pivoting direction.

4

Edison bulb with radial glow halos

Inspired by the Vecteezy reference. Yellow gradient bulb, wire, screw base, zigzag filament, soft radial glow circles.

"The rays are lost on the light background. Try a warm taupe background."

5

Warm taupe background rectangle

Added a dark warm taupe (#6b5e54) rounded rect inside the SVG. Glow circles dimmed for contrast.

"Not what I meant — the background of the entire image, not a rectangle inside it. Much warmer."

6

Vector rays + dark line art + CSS warm background

Replaced soft glow circles with crisp vector ray lines. Switched to dark line-art style with white glass bulb over yellow glow. CSS provides the warm accent-soft backdrop.

"Rays should have yellow under the black, like the light."

Final: "Yellow under black" Edison bulb

Fat yellow rays with thin dark center lines for definition. White glass bulb over yellow glow. Edison screw base with ridge lines. Zigzag filament between support wires. 13 vector rays total (7 major + 6 accent). Scales cleanly from 34px to any size.

"Yellow under black is more cohesive and looks way better in the small size. Well done!"
