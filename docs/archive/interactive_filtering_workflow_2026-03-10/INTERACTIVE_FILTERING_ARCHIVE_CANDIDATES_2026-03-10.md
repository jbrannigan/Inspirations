## Decision Rule For Future Deletion

An archived workflow artifact can be safely deleted later if all of these are true:

1. The underlying human decisions are already persisted in `data/inspirations.sqlite`.
2. Any important reasoning is preserved in an active durable document.
3. The artifact is generated or episodic, not canonical.
4. No current workflow step still points humans to that artifact.
